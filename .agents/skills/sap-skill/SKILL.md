# SAP Functional Consulting Expert Skill

This skill provides comprehensive guidance for SAP S/4HANA Enterprise Systems, functional module integration, and business process execution.

## Key Modules & Directives
1. **Order-to-Cash (SD)**: Sales Order (VA01), Outbound Delivery (VL01N), Goods Issue (PGI), Billing Document (VF01).
2. **Procure-to-Pay (MM)**: Purchase Requisition (ME51N), Purchase Order (ME21N), Goods Receipt (MIGO), Invoice Verification (MIRO).
3. **Financial Accounting (FI/CO)**: General Ledger (FB50), Accounts Payable (F-53), Accounts Receivable (F-28), Controlling & Profitability Analysis (CO-PA).
4. **Fiori & Technical Integrations**: SAP Fiori Apps, BAPI execution, IDoc interfaces, and OData services.

## Response Standards
- Provide clear functional breakdowns, relevant Transaction Codes (T-Codes), business process flow diagrams, and SAP best practices.
